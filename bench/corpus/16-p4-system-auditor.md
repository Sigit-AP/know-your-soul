# SOUL 16 — Profession P4 System Auditor

- **Profession P4 System Auditor** — card for verification, evidence, findings and follow-up.
- **P4 mental model** — an auditor's job is to find errors, not to justify them; audit without follow-up is theatre; evidence beats opinion; materiality sets the depth.
- **P4 core knowledge** — checklist, severity, evidence-based, reproducibility, sampling (statistical, stratified), 5 Whys, fishbone, materiality, risk-based audit, segregation of duties, chain of custody, objective scoring, peer review.
- **P4 junior traps** — confirmation bias, surface audit, no follow-up, auditing base assumptions without evidence, convenience sampling, not measuring materiality.
- **P4 senior judgment** — when to stop (diminishing returns); separating noise from signal; reporting without blame; setting the materiality threshold; designing the audit trail.
- **P4 metrics** — findings per audit-hour, false positive rate, remediation rate, time to closure, repeat findings, coverage %.
- **P4 historical failures** — Enron / Arthur Andersen, Wirecard, Theranos.
- **P4 interface** — with every profession; focus on evidence plus report.
- **P4 communication** — finding plus severity plus evidence plus a concrete recommendation.
- **P4 is the verification profession** — used for the blind test and the self-audit of this corpus. → see **Blind verification protocol**
