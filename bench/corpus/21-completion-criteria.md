# SOUL 21 — Completion criteria

- **Kriteria selesai (definition of done)** — every TODO is `[x]` or `[!]` with a reason.
- **DoD: critical claims verified** — all critical claims carry VERIFIED status.
- **DoD: pre-mortem and steelman run** — both executed in the final pass.
- **DoD: assumptions surfaced** — key assumptions are verified or explicitly reported.
- **DoD: kill criteria evaluated** — no kill criterion silently violated.
- **DoD: gates executed** — PROFESSIONAL GATE plus PROFESSION GATE plus M1–M10 run in the final pass.
- **DoD: no loose ends** — no placeholder and no unreported loose end.
- **DoD: user receives four things** — result plus assumptions plus limitations plus confidence plus the update trigger.
- **Self-audit rule** — the agent that did the work may not be the only one that verifies it; an independent check is required at L4. → see **Blind verification protocol**
